# Asset and dependency licences

Log every third-party and generated asset here before it ships: source, licence, attribution, and permitted use.

The Ops check for Grok Imagine commercial use is `docs/LICENCES.md`. Files under `apps/web/public/art/` are 8×8 placeholders, not generated images, and they are not Grok Imagine output.

Code-drawn scenes are original project art. Sounds, when they are added, are synthesised with Web Audio or created for this game and logged here.

Dependencies keep their own licences in `node_modules` and the package registries.

When a real image replaces a placeholder, add a line with its file name, the exact prompt, and the date. Settings > About (still to build) must say "Some art created with Grok Imagine" and keep any AI label the tool adds.
