Privacy policy

Sweet Home Reset, by PixelVortex Games. Draft of 30 October 2026, to be reviewed before release.

The short version

  • The game saves your town on your own device. It plays fully offline, with no account.
  • There are no ads, no ad IDs and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.
  • An account is optional (16 and over). It only exists to move your town between devices and keep a purchase with you.
  • You can download or delete everything we keep, from inside the game.

On your device

Your town, settings and a backup copy are stored on your device. They never leave it unless you sign in to an account.

If you make an account

We keep your email address (or your Apple ID's sign-in, which can be a private relay address), a copy of your save, up to 10 older copies for 30 days, and whether you own the full game. Sign-in links are sent by email through Resend. We store only scrambled (hashed) versions of sign-in links and sessions.

Buying the full game

On the web, payment is handled by Stripe; we never see your card details. In the iOS and Android apps, the App Store or Google Play handles payment, and RevenueCat tells us the full game was bought for your account. We keep that you own it, when and where it was bought.

Anonymous gameplay counts

The game can send a few anonymous counts, such as which in-game day was started, so we can see whether people come back and enjoy it. They carry a random install number, never your name, email or account, and are kept for 13 months. You can turn them off in the game's settings.

Who helps us run it

Cloudflare hosts the game and its server. Resend sends sign-in emails. Stripe, Apple, Google and RevenueCat handle purchases. Each only gets what it needs for that job.

Children

The game itself is made for everyone. Accounts are for people 16 and over, and purchases sit behind a grown-up check.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Pause, then Account, then Export my data.
  • Delete your account: Pause, then Account, then Delete account. Your cloud save and history are deleted straight away and the account within 30 days. The delete-account page explains this too.
  • Store purchases stay with your App Store or Google Play account; refunds are handled by Apple or Google.
