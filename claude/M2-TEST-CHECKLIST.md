# M2 test checklist (phone test, step 10)

Test link: https://blake-caligari123.github.io/Sweet-Home-reset/test/. Reload once after each deploy. Start with a new game (Pause → Restart).

## 1. The first 30 minutes (step 8)
- [ ] The tips go in order:
  1. walk to the rug
  2. dust 3 spots
  3. put the sock in the basket
  4. sponge the stain
  5. eat the snack on the counter
  6. tidy the rest of the burrow
- [ ] The burrow restores with a reveal and the "Before and after" offer.
- [ ] In the yard: rake 3 weeds, plant 3 radish seeds, water them. Watering is free.
- [ ] Walk into the square. Puff says "Why is that fountain so quiet?"
- [ ] Say hello to Hazel in the bakery and get a Berry tart. Eat it from the Bag.
- [ ] Take Hazel's counter job from the notice board, then dust her counter.
- [ ] Meet Bea and Pip on Neighbour Lane, through the square's east gate. If Pip's asleep, he says when he's up.
- [ ] Day 1 lasts about 10 minutes; later days about 8. Near 1 am Puff says "Head home to bed."
- [ ] Sleep. The Day 2 letter mentions what you cleaned.
- [ ] Day 2: the radishes have sprouted and the General Store is open.
- [ ] Doing things out of order (for example, Bea before Hazel) never gets the tips stuck.

## 2. Fluff balance (new)
- [ ] A clean hit costs 1 Fluff. Restoring the burrow leaves about half a bar.
- [ ] Fluff lasts most of the day, with snacks helping. It shouldn't run out in the first few minutes.
- [ ] Under 20 Fluff, Puff yawns and cleaning costs a little more.

## 3. Neighbours walking about (step 1)
- [ ] **Hazel:** bakes in the morning, runs the shop by day, and dozes in her chair at night.
- [ ] **Bea:** at her shop 8–6, does lists in the back room around 1 pm, and walks to Riverside at 6 pm.
- [ ] **Pip:** sleeps until 10, works at his bench, and visits Hazel's around 3 pm.
- [ ] Neighbours walk through doors rather than popping in and out.

## 4. Talking (step 2)
- [ ] The first chat each day gives +1 friendship. Talking again gives a short "busy" line.
- [ ] Lines change with the time of day and over the days, with no instant repeats.

## 5. Gifts and hearts (step 3)
- [ ] "Give a gift" works once a day per neighbour.
- [ ] A loved gift gets a big reaction, a liked one a nice one, and a disliked one a kind "not for me" that costs nothing.
- [ ] Pause → ♥ Friends shows hearts, progress, and the gifts you've found they like.
- [ ] Hearts never go down, even after a reload.

## 6. Heart scenes (step 4)
- [ ] A scene plays the next time you talk after reaching 2 hearts. It can be skipped, and choices work.
- [ ] Loved gifts are the fastest way to hearts: flowers for Hazel, shells for Otto, a book for Moss.

## 7. Notice board and jobs (step 5)
- [ ] 1–3 jobs a day, and you can hold up to 3.
- [ ] Tidy jobs put fresh mess down. "Bring" jobs show a "Give the …" button in that neighbour's chat.
- [ ] Finishing a job gives Sparkles and friendship, and the tracker updates.
- [ ] Missed jobs quietly go after 2–3 days, with no penalty.

## 8. Shops (step 6)
- [ ] **General Store,** off the square: open from day 2, 8 am–6 pm.
  - Buy seeds, an oat biscuit and a bigger bag.
  - Sell radishes.
  - The Copper sponge recipe appears from day 5.
- [ ] **Bea's flowers:** use "Browse flowers" in her chat while her shop is open.
- [ ] You can't buy when the shop is closed or you're short of Sparkles.

## 9. New areas (step 7)
- [ ] **Bea's shop and Pip's workshop** each restore with a reveal and +100 Sparkles. Lists go to Bea's desk, and toys go in Pip's toy box.
- [ ] **Moss's library,** at the end of the Lane: dust, cobwebs, and books for the shelves.
- [ ] **Riverside,** through the Lane's bottom gate: closed until day 3.
  - Two shells on the bank each morning.
  - The beach path on the right says it's overgrown.
- [ ] **Otto's bathhouse:** soap scum, foggy patches, and towels for the basket.
- [ ] Restored homes stay restored after a reload.

## 10. Otto, Moss and Mabel (7c)
- [ ] **Otto:** on the jetty at dawn, in the bathhouse 10–4, and on the jetty again at night.
- [ ] **Moss:** in his library. He's asleep before 10 am.
- [ ] **Mabel:** walks into the square around 6 pm and sits by the east bench until 1 am.
- [ ] Their jobs show up on the board. Otto's only appear from day 3.

## 11. Phase 0: names, Mabel, sleepy bar
- [ ] A new game asks for names first (Puff and Hollowfern are filled in). Your names show in the tracker, Puff's lines, the letter sign-off, area names ("…'s burrow") and the share text.
- [ ] Pause → ✎ Names changes them. An empty box goes back to the usual name.
- [ ] Mabel isn't in the square before day 43, and none of her jobs appear until then. If you met her in an older test save, she's still there.
- [ ] Otto and Moss are there from the start, in colour (no silhouettes).
- [ ] Below 20 Fluff, the bar turns peach and breathes gently, and Puff says "Feeling sleepy... a snack would help." With Reduce motion on, it stays still.
- [ ] An old save loads with all its progress and the usual names.

## 11b. Phase 1a: the Keeper and every place coming alive
- [ ] The first time you talk to Hazel, she names Puff the Keeper. Four short boxes, skippable, then "🏅 [name], Keeper of [town]!". It only plays once.
- [ ] Every home comes alive as you tidy it (about ¼, ½, ¾ and restored), each with a chime, a sparkle and a line from Puff:
  - lamps, sunbeams, decor, the library cat, robins
  - Outdoors: lamps, flowers and birds in the yard, square, Lane and Riverside.
- [ ] Leave and come back: what's switched on stays on.

## 11c. Phase 1b: the Keeper's plans
- [ ] In the square, the blue plans board sits right of the fountain. Before Hazel's welcome it says "Keeper wanted". After it, on day 1: "Plans start tomorrow".
- [ ] Day 2: the tracker points to the plans. The board lists 4 plots, with what fits each and its cost. Anything you can't afford is greyed out.
- [ ] Build a bench by the fountain: Sparkles go down, and scaffolding appears straight away.
- [ ] Sleep (or doze off). Next morning, "The new bench is finished. Come see!", and the bench is there. You can sit beside it, and nothing blocks the doors or the board.
- [ ] Reload mid-build: the scaffolding and the Sparkles stay as they were.

## 11d. Phase 1c: dust Grumbles
- [ ] Before day 3: no Grumbles anywhere.
- [ ] Day 3: the tracker says something grey is grumbling on Neighbour Lane. Walk in and Puff says "You look like me!"
- [ ] There are 1–3 Grumbles each on the Lane, in Pip's workshop and in Moss's library. They amble slowly and shuffle away if Puff gets close.
- [ ] Tap one: Puff dusts it from beside it (1 Fluff) and a pip fills. Its face cheers up with each go: droopy, then wide-eyed, then winking. It holds still while you tidy it. Three goes and it turns butter-yellow, with "Friend made! ✨". Tapping it again says "Pff! Ooh!"
- [ ] Sleep: untidied ones are gone and new ones arrive. Your helpers stay. Nothing is lost.
- [ ] Reload mid-tidy: the pips, positions and helpers are all kept.
- [ ] Dragging the sock to its basket still works.

## 11e. Phase 1d: your pet
- [ ] Before day 4, nothing happens at the door.
- [ ] Day 4: step out of the burrow into the yard. "Someone's at the door!" and a choice of cat, pup or hedgehoglet appears.
- [ ] "Maybe later" closes it. The tracker says someone's waiting, and it comes back the next day.
- [ ] Choose one and name it (its usual name is filled in). You get "Welcome home, [name]!"
- [ ] It follows Puff everywhere, round corners and through doors, and faces the way it's going.
- [ ] Tap it: sparkles and a happy line.
- [ ] Reload: your pet and its name are still there.

## 11f. Phase 1e: new gestures and mess drifting back
- [ ] **Hold:** keep a finger on a stain or cobweb and Puff keeps scrubbing until it's gone. On a Grumble, it tidies it all the way.
- [ ] **Circle:** draw circles over a stain to scrub it with suds.
- [ ] **Sweep:** swipe along a row of dust or weeds and Puff tidies each one in turn.
- [ ] A back-and-forth rub still works. Dragging the sock to its basket still carries it. A drag over empty floor just walks.
- [ ] Tapping alone still does everything.
- [ ] Sleep after restoring a room: next day there are a couple of fresh dust bits (or weeds outside). The room stays restored and keeps everything that came alive. It never gets more than 4.

## 11g. Phase 2a: forage, Pip's workbench, Copper tools
- [ ] Twigs and pebbles lie about in the yard, square, Lane and Riverside. Tap to pick them up. They're back each morning.
- [ ] The General Store sells the Copper duster and sponge recipes from day 5, and the rake recipe from day 7 (100 ✨ each). Buying one: "Pip can help me make it at the workbench!"
- [ ] In Pip's workshop, tap his table to open the workbench. It shows what each recipe needs and how many you have, and Make works once you've got it all.
- [ ] A Copper tool gets a copper rim on the toolbar and cleans in fewer goes. The Copper sponge cleans grime.
- [ ] Hold on mess with a Copper tool: "Whoosh! A big clean!" and the spot plus up to 2 matching spots beside it clear. It costs 5 Fluff.

## 12b. Phase 4b: the seasons turn
- [ ] Day 15 brings "Summer in Hollowfern! New seeds at the shops.", then autumn on day 29, winter on day 43, and spring again on day 57 (a new year).
- [ ] Outdoors: summer looks like spring with warmer light; in autumn the greens turn golden with falling leaves; winter is snowy, with snowflakes instead of petals or fireflies.
- [ ] Crops grow only in their seasons:
  - radish in spring
  - carrot in spring and autumn
  - peas and strawberries in spring and summer
  - tomato and corn in summer
  - pumpkin in autumn
- [ ] Out of season a crop just rests and keeps its progress. With a finished greenhouse, the yard grows anything any time.
- [ ] Shops only sell seeds that grow that season (none outdoors in winter). Planting seeds out of season says they rest till their season.
- [ ] Each neighbour has a line for summer, autumn and winter.
