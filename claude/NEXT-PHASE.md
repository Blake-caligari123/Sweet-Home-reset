# Sweet Home Reset: from basic to deep (Phase 6, the road to early access)
*Blake and Claude Code, 30 Oct 2026. Built from Blake's 100 answers (the log is `claude/PHASE-2-ANSWERS.md`). This extends `claude/VISION.md`: where the two differ, this file wins. Where Blake said "dealer's choice", the call is marked **(Claude's call)** so it can be changed.*

## 1. Why
Today the game works but feels basic in every way Blake named: there's too little to do, everything feels the same, nothing really matters, and it looks and sounds plain. The fix isn't more of the same. It's **depth that connects**: everything Puff does feeds something else, the town and its people react, and the world looks and sounds alive.

**The feeling we want:** the classic cosy farm-life "just one more day". A cosy routine with a surprise every day. Gentle by default, but the more things you try, the more the game gives back. You can still spend a whole day just farming or just tidying, and that's fine.

## 2. Pillars (updated)
1. **Colour comes back.** (kept) The reward is the world coming alive.
2. **Satisfying in your fingers.** (kept) Every gesture feels good.
3. **Everything connects.** (new) Tidying, farming, cooking, crafting, friends and building all feed each other. No system is an island.
4. **The town is alive.** (new) About 20 townsfolk with routines, moods, opinions, memories and friendships, who notice what Puff does.
5. **Gentle, never a grind.** (sharpened) Nothing is lost or failed. Never repeat a chore just to push a number up. Timers, harder challenges and longer scenes are fine in the right place (a festival game, a cave puzzle, a big story moment). There's no fighting that harms anyone.
6. **Always more.** (kept) Free updates add districts and chapters.

## 4. How everything connects
```
            ┌────────── finds, stories, recipe cards ──────────┐
 TIDY ──► materials ──► CRAFT / COOK ──► gifts, dishes, furniture ──► TOWNSFOLK
  ▲                         ▲                                         │
  │                    crops, milk, eggs ◄── FARM ◄── seeds, tools ◄──┤ quests, tokens, recipes
  │                                                                   ▼
 new mess, new homes ◄── NEW DISTRICT ◄── big projects ◄── TOWN NEEDS + town fund
```
- **Tidying** turns up materials, finds, recipe cards and (in special homes) story pieces.
- **Farming** gives ingredients, gifts, things to sell, and fibre, wood and dyes to build with.
- **Cooking** turns crops into dishes: boosts, gifts, café stock and quest items.
- **Crafting** turns materials into tools, furniture, farm kit and handmade gifts.
- **Townsfolk** want things (quests, gifts, help mending their friendships). They pay in Sparkles, their own tokens, recipes and friendship, and they change once their homes are tidy.
- **Town needs** ("somewhere to eat", "clean water") point at what to build next. Working buildings meet the needs, the town fund grows, and big projects open **new districts**, which bring new people, new mess and new places.

## 5. The systems

### 5.1 Tidying 2.0
- **Each place has its own mess:** flour and burnt crumbs in the bakery, soap scum and steam in the bathhouse, ink and paper in the library, oil and sawdust in the workshop, sand and seaweed at the beach, and more.
- **Homes are puzzles** too: sort, straighten and put things where they belong. Getting it right gives a bonus.
- **Combos:** tidying spots in a quick chain within a room builds a gentle streak with growing sparkle and a bigger reward. It's never needed.
- **Hidden things under mess:** finds, recipe cards, Grumble nests, keys to secret spots.
- **Tools with special moves:** each tier and tool changes how you clean (sweep a row, burst a patch, blow away cobwebs, steam a window).
- **Special homes** hold a story told through things found while tidying (notes, photos, memories).
- **Tidying changes the people:** a restored home lifts its owner's mood and opens new lines, and afterwards they join in and help tidy.

### 5.2 Puff
- **Skills** level up by doing: Tidying, Farming, Cooking, Crafting, Fishing and Exploring. Each level gives a perk (a free charged swing a day, better crop quality, a new recipe slot). Skills grow from variety, never from grinding one chore.
- **New moves** open through the year: hop over small gaps, roll down slopes, float on a breeze, squeeze through small holes. These open secrets and shortcuts.
- **Moods** with small effects: a happy Puff tidies a little faster, a curious Puff finds more, and a sleepy Puff says so. Moods come from food, sleep, a cosy home and friends. Puff is never sad because of the player.
- **Movement:** tap to walk or a virtual joystick, the player picks. **Getting around was Blake's least favourite part**, so it gets fixed first (§9).
- **Dress-up:** lots of outfits, accessories and fur tints, all earned in play (cosmetic packs later; §10).

### 5.3 Townsfolk (about 20)
- **Alive:**
  - Each has a daily and weekly routine that weather and seasons change.
  - Moods come from their needs (food, rest, a tidy home, friends).
  - They have opinions about the town and each other.
  - They have a memory of what Puff did, which they bring up later.
- **Friendships and small dramas** between them. Two neighbours fall out over the noise from the workshop, and Puff can help them make up. These run as small storylines.
- **Bigger quests:** multi-day quest chains with a story and a real reward, alongside the daily notice board.
- **Birthdays and anniversaries:** each has a birthday, there's a town founding day, and newcomers have moving-in days.
- **Babble voices:** each character has their own babble sound.
- **Who arrives:** the six and Sorrel first, then about 13 newcomers across the districts (a café owner, a fisher, a farmer with animals, a carpenter, a musician, a postie, a gardener, a child or two with a parent, and others). Each has a home to tidy, a job in the town economy and a heart arc.

### 5.4 The town
- **Town needs:** a clear list ("somewhere to eat", "clean water", "a place to rest", "music in the square"). Meeting a need makes the town happier, unlocks more and moves the story on.
- **Working buildings** with their own small economy:
  - The café serves dishes Puff supplies.
  - The well brings water to nearby crops.
  - The market stall sells what Puff stocks.
  - The workshop takes orders.
  - The bakery buys flour.
  - Homes are homes.
- **Layout:** set plots for big buildings, and free placement for small things (paths, fences, lamps, benches, flower beds).
- **New districts** open through big projects: each brings townsfolk, buildings, mess, critters and secrets (§7).
- **Getting around:** map travel to places you've been, plus shortcuts to unlock (paths, a cart, a ferry).

### 5.5 Home
- **The burrow:** full decorating (furniture anywhere, wallpaper, floors, rugs, lights), plus **rooms that unlock** and get filled (kitchen, craft room, bedroom, a cellar, and more).
- **Neighbours' homes:** Puff can decorate to each neighbour's taste. Getting it right makes them happy. An **auto-decorate** option does it for players who'd rather not.
- **Furniture** is crafted, bought and found.
- **A cosy home gives small bonuses:** better sleep, a little more Fluff, more visitors.

### 5.6 Farm
- **Big farming:** soil care, fertiliser, crop quality, the odd giant crop, crops by season, a greenhouse.
- **Animals at 1.0:** hens, a goat and a cow, cared for gently. They give eggs, milk and wool.
- **Growth:** clear land to expand the farm, and upgrade beds and kit (sprinklers, beehives, a scarecrow).
- **Crops are for everything:** cooking, gifts and quests, selling, and building and crafting.

### 5.7 Cooking and crafting
- **Cooking:** a big recipe book to fill, and cooking with gestures (chop, stir, knead, bake). Dishes give **small boosts** for the day (faster tidying, more Fluff, better finds).
- **Crafting:** tools, furniture and decor, farm kit, and handmade gifts.
- **Recipes come from** experimenting, neighbours as friendship grows, cards found while tidying, and shops.

### 5.8 Money and trade
- **Sparkles** (money), **materials**, **neighbour tokens** (each friend or shop has thank-you tokens for special items) and a **town fund** (townsfolk chip in for big projects).
- **Shops:** stock rotates by day and season, with travelling specials, and grows as shops are upgraded.
- **Selling:** Puff's own market stall on market days (set prices, customers come by), selling to shops any day, and orders to fill.
- **Money curve:** tight early, easier later. Saving up for big things feels good.

### 5.9 Exploring and things to do
- **A big, rich world** with lots of secrets: hidden paths, secret rooms, things only curious players find (some need Puff's new moves).
- **Caves:** mining and ore, with gentle puzzles and the town's history deeper down.
- **Fishing** (river, pond, sea) and **critter catching** (catch and release) at 1.0, changing by season, weather and time of day.
- **Many small mini-games** around town, plus the festival games.
- **Wildlife** that visits when the town suits it (birds, hedgehogs, frogs).
- **Grumbles:** 10+ kinds (one per mess type and season, some rare). Collect them and give befriended ones jobs: water crops, tidy a room, fetch materials. This is how chores get automated instead of ground through.
- **The pet:** a companion that helps, sniffing out hidden finds and digging up treasure.
- **Collections** (Moss's museum): finds, fish, critters, recipes, Grumbles and wildlife, with rewards for each finished set.

### 5.10 Time, seasons and the calendar
- **Days:** 10 minutes (§3). **Seasons:** 28 days (§3).
- **Night activities:** stargazing, fireflies, night critters and fish, a late café, lantern walks.
- **Seasons change play a lot:** each brings its own mess, crops, fish, critters, activities and events.
- **Weather:** many kinds, each with effects (rain, wind, storms, snow, fog, a heatwave).
- **Festivals:** four big ones with prep days (decorations go up, stalls, several games on the day), plus small monthly events.
- **Real-world links:** small specials on real holidays and a small daily treat (never a streak). Game time stays separate from real time.
- **The daily goals list** is optional: 3–4 small goals with a small reward.
- **Long goals** are always clear in the tracker, which can be hidden.

### 5.11 Story
- **Woven together:** a main story in chapters (one per season), the fountain mystery underneath, and each townsperson's own story, all touching.
- **Choices with real branches:** some choices change outcomes, who moves in, how a drama ends, or what the fountain reveals. Branches are tracked in save flags.
- **Shown as a mix:** illustrated scenes for big moments, dialogue for everyday talk, and letters, notes and finds.
- **Tone:** funny day to day, moving at big moments, a mystery underneath.

### 5.12 Look and sound
- **Less flat:**
  - Light and shadow: day-night lighting, lamps, shadows, depth.
  - More animation: characters, trees, water, smoke.
  - Richer detail in every corner.
  - Height and layers: hills, stairs, bridges, things passing in front and behind.
- **Pinch to zoom.** **Moderate feedback** (clear but calm). **Pixel-style menus** to match the world.
- **Music** per place and season, growing richer as the town comes alive. **Rich ambient sound** (birds, wind, water, chatter, footsteps by surface).
- **More haptics,** with an off switch.

### 5.13 Friends and sharing
- **Visit friends' towns at 1.0:** walk around, leave a gift, sign a guestbook with preset stamps. There's no chat, and visits are opt-in, so it's safe for kids. This needs accounts (built) and a visits server.
- **Photo mode** (frames, stickers, poses, hide the HUD), **before/after clips** and **town postcards**.
- **Friendly comparing only:** festival dash scores with friends.
- **Community events** (town-wide goals across all players) later.

### 5.14 Wellbeing and access
- **Real chores, grown:** more kinds and a cosy log of what you did. A chore gives a small boost and, now and then, a cosmetic.
- **Calm extras:** sit-and-watch spots, a breathe-with-Puff moment, a lo-fi radio.
- **Notifications:** gentle and opt-in ("your crops are ready", "it's Bea's birthday"), never guilt.
- **A simple-words setting** for young readers. **Learning:** a short guided start, then a tip the first time you meet each thing. **Tracker:** clear and hideable.

## 6. Never
- **Grinding.** Every system has a way out of repetition: helpers (Grumbles, neighbours who join in) take over chores, skills grow from variety, and rewards shrink for doing the same thing all day while the game nudges somewhere new.
- Still never (CLAUDE.md hard rules): ads, tracking, loot boxes, paid timers, resetting streaks, paid power.
