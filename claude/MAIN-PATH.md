# The Keeper's Story: the guided main path (plan, 31 Oct 2026)

For Blake's yes before building. It comes from your answers:
- **What feels wrong:** you don't know what to do next, the features feel separate, and the story is hard to follow.
- **How firm:** a gentle main path. There's always one clear next step, and you can ignore it.
- **Who guides:** neighbours ask for things, along a main story quest line. There's a Keeper's journal, and the fountain speaks at special moments.
- **Scope:** the whole year from day 1. The quests already built are folded in. About one step a day. The fountain only speaks at chapter beats.

## How it works

1. **The Keeper's Story** is one quest line through the year, in five chapters: a prologue and one per season. Each chapter ends at its festival.
2. **One step a day.**
   - Each morning the next step is ready. It takes a few minutes, and the rest of the day is free.
   - If Puff skips a day, the step just waits. Nothing is missed or lost.
   - If Puff already did the thing (fished before Otto asked), the step ticks itself off and the next one is ready tomorrow.
3. **A person asks for every step.**
   - The step shows in the tracker (📖) and as a button in that neighbour's chat box.
   - Each feature is introduced by a friend: Otto shows you fishing, Fig the animals, Pip the workbench, Hazel the kitchen, and so on.
   - That neighbour's line also points to the next person, so the town feels connected.
4. **Existing quests become stops on the path.**
   - Hazel's fair prep, Moss's reading club, Fig's garden, Wren's sail, the summer and autumn chapters and the festival preps are now main-path steps.
   - Anything not on the path stays as an optional side quest in the journal.
5. **The Keeper's journal** (Pause → 📔 Journal) has four pages:
   - **Today:** the main step, plus up to 2 "you could also…" ideas (a job, the café order, a new critter season).
   - **This chapter:** the story so far, written as short diary lines that fill in as you go (all the clues about Thimble in one place).
   - **New to try:** features that have opened but you haven't used yet. This replaces the scattered tracker nudges.
   - **Side quests:** the ones you're on and the ones on offer.
6. **The fountain** speaks only at chapter beats: a glow and two or three lines when a chapter opens and when it closes at the festival. That's five or six moments a year, so it stays special.
7. **Nothing is locked by the path.** Places, shops and people still open on their days as now. The path just makes sure you're pointed at each one when it arrives.

## What changes in the code
- `story/`: the Keeper's Story steps as pack data (`story` in the pack: day, who, goal, line). Goals reuse the quest ones, so a step can be a talk, tidy, cook, fish, find, build or quest. It's one daily step, and it can tick itself off from things already done.
- `journal/`: the 📔 Journal sheet with its four pages.
- Fountain moments: a short scene at chapter open and close, using the story scenes that already exist.
- Tracker: the 📖 main step first. The scattered nudges in `play/nudges.ts` move into the journal's "New to try".
- **Timing moves:** Juniper's letter goes from day 40 to day 31, and the old song chapter from day 44 to day 37 (needing only Juniper at home and the hill open, not the concert scene).
- A Feature tour card per chapter, which jumps to its first day.
