# The Keeper's Story: the guided main path (plan, 31 Oct 2026)

For Blake's yes before building. It comes from your answers:
- **What feels wrong:** you don't know what to do next, the features feel separate, and the story is hard to follow.
- **How firm:** a gentle main path. There's always one clear next step, and you can ignore it.
- **Who guides:** neighbours ask for things, along a main story quest line. There's a Keeper's journal, and the fountain speaks at special moments.
- **Scope:** the whole year from day 1. The quests already built are folded in. About one step a day. The fountain only speaks at chapter beats.

## How it works

1. **The Keeper's Story** is one quest line through the year, in five chapters: a prologue and one per season. Each chapter ends at its festival.
2. **One step a day.**
   - Each morning the next step is ready. It takes a few minutes, and the rest of the day is free.
   - If Puff skips a day, the step just waits. Nothing is missed or lost.
   - If Puff already did the thing (fished before Otto asked), the step ticks itself off and the next one is ready tomorrow.
3. **A person asks for every step.**
   - The step shows in the tracker (📖) and as a button in that neighbour's chat box.
   - Each feature is introduced by a friend: Otto shows you fishing, Fig the animals, Pip the workbench, Hazel the kitchen, and so on.
   - That neighbour's line also points to the next person, so the town feels connected.
4. **Existing quests become stops on the path.**
   - Hazel's fair prep, Moss's reading club, Fig's garden, Wren's sail, the summer and autumn chapters and the festival preps are now main-path steps.
   - Anything not on the path stays as an optional side quest in the journal.
5. **The Keeper's journal** (Pause → 📔 Journal) has four pages:
   - **Today:** the main step, plus up to 2 "you could also…" ideas (a job, the café order, a new critter season).
   - **This chapter:** the story so far, written as short diary lines that fill in as you go (all the clues about Thimble in one place).
   - **New to try:** features that have opened but you haven't used yet. This replaces the scattered tracker nudges.
   - **Side quests:** the ones you're on and the ones on offer.
6. **The fountain** speaks only at chapter beats: a glow and two or three lines when a chapter opens and when it closes at the festival. That's five or six moments a year, so it stays special.
7. **Nothing is locked by the path.** Places, shops and people still open on their days as now. The path just makes sure you're pointed at each one when it arrives.

## Cutscenes (Blake, 31 Oct: "some cutscenes at the start to introduce it")
Your answers: played out in the town itself, about a minute at the start, plus chapter openings, newcomers arriving and the big reveals.

**How they play:**
- The game world acts the scene out. The camera glides between places, characters walk and turn, colour drains or floods back, and short captions sit along the bottom (60 characters each, kind voice).
- Tap moves to the next caption, and a **Skip** button is always there.
- With reduced motion the camera cuts instead of gliding, and nothing bobs.
- Every scene can be watched again from the journal ("Memories").

### The opening (built: one continuous take, about 60 seconds)
Remade twice after Blake's notes: first "fluid and make sense, not just random shots", then "the story makes sense but not what's going on in the cutscene". Now every caption has something on screen showing it:
1. **The square, long ago:** the fountain glows warm and sparkles, and Thimble (from behind) hums ♪ beside it while the neighbours stroll. *"Long ago, {town} glowed with colour." / "Thimble, its Keeper, kept the fountain's water warm."*
2. **Snow begins:** Thimble yawns 💤 and climbs down into the fountain. *"One winter, Thimble grew very, very sleepy..." / "...and climbed down inside the fountain."*
3. **The Old Spring, deep below:** Thimble curls up by the glowing pool and sleeps 💤 while the glow fades. *"Deep below, by the warm spring, Thimble curled up." / "Thimble slept, and the spring slowly cooled."*
4. **The square:** the fountain's glow goes out, the grey ripples outward, and the neighbours yawn 💤 and doze. *"Up above, the fountain's glow went out." / "The colour slipped away. Everyone grew sleepy."*
5. **Dusk:** one sparkle pops out of the fountain and drifts out of the square. *"But the fountain kept one last sparkle." / "It drifted off, looking for somewhere cosy."*
6. **The yard:** along the path to the burrow door. *"Past the hedge, across a quiet little yard..."*
7. **The burrow:** it settles by the bed as a sleeping Puff, who wakes with a "!". *"...and in through a round burrow door." / "It settled by the bed, and turned into... you!" / "Good morning, little puff!"*

### Chapter openings (about 20 seconds each, on the chapter's first morning)
The fountain glows. The camera drifts to where the chapter happens, then back, and the fountain line plays.
- **Spring, day 4:** the grey square and the first coloured spots. *"You found me. The town remembers colour, one spot at a time."*
- **Summer, day 15:** a glide across the river to the Far Bank, the boathouse roof just showing. *"Something old waits across the water."*
- **Autumn, day 29:** the woods, with leaves falling and a faint four-note hum. *"Listen in the woods. The town has a song."*
- **Winter, day 43:** snow on the caves' mouth and a warm glow deep inside. *"Thimble is sleeping by the warm spring. Light the town, and they'll wake."*
- **Chapter endings** at each festival get a short closing beat too (lines in the day-by-day tables).

### Newcomers arriving (about 15 seconds, the morning the cottage is finished)
The camera finds the new cottage, and the newcomer walks up with a bag, looks around and waves. A caption in their voice follows, then a banner.
- **Sorrel:** *"A home! A real one! Hello, {town}!"*
- **Fig:** arrives with a seed box. *"Good soil. I can tell already."*
- **Wren:** Pip runs to meet them. *"Wren! You came back!"*
- **Juniper:** plays the four notes on the doorstep. *"There it is. The old tune."*

### Big reveals
- **The boathouse:** the hedge parts, then a slow push in on the boat and the carving.
- **The humming stone:** on Orchard Hill at dusk, with the stone faintly glowing as Juniper plays.
- **Thimble's last carving:** in the deepest cave.
- **Winter Lights:** the lanterns light one by one across town, the fountain blazes, and Thimble's voice wakes.

**In the code:** a small scene player (`story/scenes.ts`) runs a list of beats from the pack. Each beat can pan the camera, move or turn a character, change the colour, set the time of day, or show a caption. It reuses the world's camera, the grey overlay and the characters already there.

## What changes in the code
- `story/`: the Keeper's Story steps as pack data (`story` in the pack: day, who, goal, line). Goals reuse the quest ones, so a step can be a talk, tidy, cook, fish, find, build or quest. It's one daily step, and it can tick itself off from things already done.
- `journal/`: the 📔 Journal sheet with its four pages.
- Fountain moments: a short scene at chapter open and close, using the story scenes that already exist.
- Tracker: the 📖 main step first. The scattered nudges in `play/nudges.ts` move into the journal's "New to try".
- **Timing moves:** Juniper's letter goes from day 40 to day 31, and the old song chapter from day 44 to day 37 (needing only Juniper at home and the hill open, not the concert scene).
- A Feature tour card per chapter, which jumps to its first day.
