# "One Home" — treatment

One animated short, vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, about 36 s. Set: the real burrow painting. Star: Puff (the real sprites), animated with squash, stretch, anticipation and follow-through. No narration; almost no text: the story is told by what Puff does.

## Structures considered
1. **A single journey** (chosen): a dust bunny is born in a grey room and makes it a home, in one continuous tidy, and falls asleep in it. One subject, one goal, one turn; the ending echoes the start (grey pile close-up → warm room wide).
2. A before/after list of rooms. Rejected: it's a montage, not a story, and it's what the shorts already do.
3. A countdown to bedtime. Rejected: the clock becomes the subject instead of Puff.

## Logline and arc
A dust bunny wakes up in a cold grey burrow and discovers that everywhere it tidies, colour comes back; by bedtime, the burrow is a home.
- **Setup**: grey room, rain on the window, a dust pile twitches, and Puff pops out of it.
- **Turn**: Puff's footstep leaves a spot of colour. Puff tests it again, then sets to work.
- **Ending**: the last grey thing is the bed. Puff climbs in, the whole room floods with warmth, and Puff falls asleep. The camera pulls all the way back: one grey pile became one warm home.

## Benchmark
Learn from short wordless character animation and cosy game trailers: clear silhouettes, one action at a time, sound on every action, a held breath before the payoff. **Don't take** characters, designs, melodies or shots from any existing work.

## Native move (the medium's own trick)
Colour seeps back **from Puff's footsteps**, like ink in water: every step, sweep and touch leaves an organic bloom of colour on the grey painting. At the peak it all floods out from the bed.

## Beat sheet (100 bpm: a beat is 0.6 s, a bar 2.4 s)
- 0.0 rain on the window; the pile is still. 0.4 and 1.2 it twitches. 1.8 nothing (a held silence).
- 2.0 **pff!** Puff bursts out (eyes shut), lands 2.35, blinks awake 2.7.
- 3.0–4.2 Puff looks left, looks right, shivers.
- 4.2, 5.4, 6.0 three footsteps, each blooming colour with a glockenspiel note; at 4.8 Puff stops and looks down.
- 6.6 a delighted hop; "Ooh." 
- 7.8 the music comes in. 9.6 sweep the first pile. 10.8 the second. 12.6 flip the sock onto the head. 15.0 toss it into the basket. 16.8 hop and wipe the window: rain stops, sunbeams in, birds. 18.0 the lamp clicks on.
- 19.8 at the bed: a yawn; the music thins. 21.4–22.2 silence.
- 22.2 hop onto the bed; 22.8 tug the blanket; 23.2 the whole room floods with colour (the peak).
- 27.0 Puff curls up on the pillow; 29.5 "one home." 31.8 title. 36.0 end.

## Sound

| Discovery | rain | soft footsteps | one glockenspiel note per step; marimba on "Ooh." |
| Tidy | rain → birds (the window wipe) | poofs, sock flip and plop, glass squeak, lamp click | harp, cello pizzicato, marimba, flute, brushed snare |
| Bed | birds | a yawn | thins to one vibraphone chord, then silence |
| Flood | birds, warm air | blanket rustle | everything: harp glissando, chimes, glockenspiel, low drum |
| Ending | room tone | soft snores | flute lullaby over harp; chimes resolve on the title |
