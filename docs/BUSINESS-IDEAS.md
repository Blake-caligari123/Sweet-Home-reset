# Sweet Home Reset: Business ideas (merged by BB Manager, 27 Oct 2026)
All ideas are untested. Locked choices still apply: Capacitor + PixiJS, one RevenueCat "plus" entitlement, A$7.99/mo or A$44.99/yr (founding members A$34.99 for the first year, web only), 16+, cosmetic-only purchases, scrubbing and mini-tasks never cost Energy.

## 1. Hands-on tasks (the player helps Puff)
Scrub grime; wipe fogged windows or mirrors (breathe, fog, wipe); sweep dust into piles for Puff to hop into; pop dust bunnies (Puff's shy cousins); fold laundry; sort clutter or a junk drawer into bins; untangle cords; vacuum crumbs; power-spray patios; dust and polish until a sparkle sweep appears; water wilted plants back to life; stack dishes into a rack; peel off stickers; shoo bugs out; hand Puff the right tool. Each room mixes 3 or 4 tasks into a 3 to 5 minute session.

## 2. What makes it satisfying
- A visible clean trail on every stroke, plus a progress meter.
- Tool sounds (scrape, fizz, squeak, sparkle), with pitch climbing as you near 100%.
- Haptics tuned to each surface (glass, wood, tile).
- A slow-motion final sparkle and a 3-second before-and-after wipe.
- Puff reacts at every stage: a happy wiggle, a dance after a perfect combo, and picking up missed mess when the player is idle.

## 3. Daily pull (never punishing)
- **Mess of the Day:** a daily seeded room that everyone plays, with a Wordle-style share card. Free for all players. This is Growth's top pick.
- **Daily fresh mess:** a 30-second new mess in an old room.
- Nothing is lost for skipping a day. Cosy Days only count up, and there's a pause mode.

## 4. Weekly drops (hard requirement)
- A "Reset Sunday" themed room every week, shipped as a data pack with no app update. Example themes: a festival tent, a gamer desk, a nan's kitchen, a beach shack after a storm.
- Monthly seasonal events, such as a haunted attic for Halloween and the kitchen after Christmas lunch.
- This is our defence against copycat games and Apple Arcade (see RIVALS.md).

## 5. Rewards
Finished rooms become decor in Puff's home. Players also earn tools and skins (a pastel spray gun, Puff in a tiny apron) and outfits with Sparkles. All prices are fixed: nothing random, no loot boxes, and no paid timers to skip.

## 6. Real-chore tie-in
"Do it for real" chores are matched to the room just cleaned. Each chore is ticked off on trust, with a daily cap. Chores give Energy and Sparkle, which power optional boosts (a stronger spray, a wider sponge) and Puff's tools. Doing a chore can also bring the next room early. Puff never gets sad or guilt-trips the player.

## 7. Shareable
- An automatic 5 to 6 second before-and-after clip.
- Puff photo mode.
- A "Can you clean it faster?" challenge.
- The Mess of the Day card.
- A read-only home tour with no names and no comments.
All of these are made on the device, with no upload, and show only game rooms, never the player's real home.

## 8. Free vs Plus
- **Free:** the Mess of the Day, 3 starter rooms and seasonal events.
- **Plus:** every weekly pack and the back catalogue, the full wardrobe and tool set, unlimited replays, home decor and double Sparkles.
- The Plus offer shows after the player's first big 100% reveal.

## 9. Trust and operations (Ops)
- An in-game "Manage plan" button goes to Stripe or the app store, and Puff explains how to cancel.
- Text-only support through a "Help Puff help you" form that goes to email.
- Notifications are opt-in.
- RevenueCat stays on the free tier with no card on file, and is listed on /privacy and /notices.
- Store apps sell Plus through Apple's and Google's own billing, which take 15%. Stripe is used on the web only.
- An IARC rating is needed before any store launch.
- Apple's developer account (US$99 a year) and Google Play's (US$25 one-off) both need Blake's yes.

## 10. Audience
Adults who play cosy games and watch ASMR cleaning videos. The pitch is cosy and ad-free, and it is never marketed as a kids' game.
